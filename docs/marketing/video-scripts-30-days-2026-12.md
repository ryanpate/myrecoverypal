# 30 days of short-form video scripts (Dec 14, 2026 – Jan 12, 2027)

Written 2026-11-24 with the `viral-content-strategist` skill. The research behind the hooks and formats is in `viral-research-2026-11.md`.

## Before filming

**Why these dates.** The court picture posts run Nov 30 – Dec 13 and say no other posts go alongside them. `court-reels-later.md` says Reels come after those. So video starts Dec 14 and runs through the holidays, New Year's Eve and the first twelve days of Dry January, which is the yearly peak for sobriety content.

**The mix.** 9 court-ordered scripts (the customer focus since 2026-11-23) and 21 general recovery and sober-curious scripts. The general ones are for reach: Reels and Shorts are pushed to non-followers, and a big general audience includes some people with a court order. Judge the two groups separately (below).

**Rules that apply to every script.**

- Faceless. Hands, paper, a phone screen, b-roll, text on screen. No talking head, no other people's faces or names.
- Not first person about drinking, recovery or a court order. "I built this" is Ryan's line and is used once (Day 24).
- Court scripts follow the rules in `court-social-posts-2026-11-30.md`: never say courts accept the sheet or reports; no fear or urgency about legal trouble; no legal advice; no emoji or exclamation marks. I've kept the general scripts to the same voice so the account sounds like one account.
- Health: nothing stated as certain for everyone, no medical advice. Every body-change or quitting video carries the doctor line on screen: "Drink heavily every day? Talk to a doctor before you stop. Withdrawal can be dangerous."
- Any video about cravings, relapse or hard nights shows "988 — call or text, any time" in the last frame.
- One CTA. Links can't be tapped in Reels or TikTok, so it's said out loud and on screen as "link in bio", and the bio link is changed to match that day's destination. Court days use `utm_campaign=court`; general days use `utm_campaign=video`. Example: `https://www.myrecoverypal.com/sobriety-calculator/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=video`.
- Same clip to TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts. Captions burned in.

**Open questions from `court-reels-later.md`, still open:** voiceover by Ryan or captions only, and whether Ryan films about ten minutes of hands-and-paper footage. Every script works either way: the VO lines are also the on-screen text.

**Changed after the Instagram scan (2026-11-24).** Three scripts were swapped for formats the scan showed working (details in `viral-research-2026-11.md`): Day 18 is now a dry Dry January joke, because the tag's viral posts are comedy; Day 20 is now the DUI-lawyer "what to do this week" shape applied to meetings; Day 26 is now the "why early sobriety feels flat" explainer, the strongest recovery topic found. Two things the scan raised that are your call: the top recovery posts are on-camera faces, and the bigger accounts use comment-a-keyword CTAs ("comment SHEET and I'll send the link"), which need you or an auto-DM tool to send the links. Days 1, 20 and 26 note a keyword version to use if you set that up.

**How to judge them.** Court scripts: attendance-sheet downloads and tracker signups from `utm_campaign=court`. General scripts: shares and saves first (those drive non-follower reach), then clicks from `utm_campaign=video`. After week 2, look at 3-second hold and completion in each platform's analytics; the two "repeat the winner" slots (Days 27 and 30) re-cut whichever format won.
