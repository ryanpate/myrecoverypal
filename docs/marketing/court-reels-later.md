# Court Reels: parked for later

Saved 2026-11-23. Decision: run the eight picture posts first (`court-social-posts-2026-11-30.md`, Nov 30 – Dec 13). Come back to Reels after those have run. Scripts are not written yet.

## Why Reels

The account's followers are general recovery people. Picture posts mostly reach followers; Reels and Shorts get pushed to people who don't follow the account, which is the only way social reaches someone newly ordered to attend meetings. The same clip can go to Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

| Post | Why it works as video |
|------|-----------------------|
| 1. The free sheet | Show the sheet being printed and filled in; the product is visible in three seconds |
| 2. Who signs it | Three quick steps, hands and a clipboard |
| 6. Proof at an online meeting | A common problem with a step-by-step answer; screen and text only |
| 8. The first meeting | Quiet text-on-screen lines over a hallway shot; most reach potential |

Posts 3, 4, 5 and 7 stay as pictures. They are lists or single lines that read better as something to save.

## Constraints

- Faceless only: hands, paper, a phone screen, or text on screen with a voiceover. No talking head.
- No first-person "I was court-ordered" voice. That is not Ryan's story.
- No other people's faces or names. No claim that courts accept the sheet or reports.
- Same rules as the picture posts: no shame, fear or urgency about the viewer's legal situation.
- Links can't be tapped in a Reel, so each one says "free sheet, link in bio" out loud and on screen.

## Open questions before writing scripts

- Can the posting session produce video, or only images? If only images, either Ryan films about ten minutes of hands-and-paper footage, or the scripts are written as text-on-screen only.
- Voiceover by Ryan, or captions only?

## How to judge them

By attendance-sheet downloads and signups from the `court` campaign tag, not by views. Reel reach is mostly not this customer.
