## 1. IPCSA — Illinois Probation & Court Services Association

**To:** association1969@ipcsa.org
**Subject:** Verifying meeting-attendance cards — worth showing your members?

Hi there,

I'm Ryan Pate, founder of MyRecoveryPal, a recovery app built in Rochester, IL.
I'm writing to the association rather than to individual departments because
this is a problem your members all seem to share.

When someone under supervision hands an officer a handwritten AA or NA
attendance card, there's usually no fast way to tell whether the signature is
real.

We built something for that. The person logs each meeting they attend and
generates a PDF attendance report carrying a cryptographic fingerprint. The
officer holding that printout can paste the fingerprint at a public page on our
site and confirm in about 30 seconds — no account, no cost, no case details
exposed — that the document hasn't been altered since it was generated. Entries
that are only self-reported are labeled as self-reported right on the report, so
it never claims more certainty than it has. Any program counts: AA, NA, CA,
SMART, Refuge, LifeRing and secular.

The one-page explanation written for officers:
https://www.myrecoverypal.com/for-probation-officers/

What I'd like to ask you:

1. Would this be worth putting in front of your members — a newsletter mention,
   a conference table, whatever format you'd consider appropriate?
2. More important: what would a report like this need before an Illinois
   probation office would actually accept it? I'd rather build to what your
   members need than guess.

Nothing for a department to buy or set up. The person under supervision
subscribes on their own ($29.99/mo); the office just receives documentation it
can check.

Thank you for the work your members do,
