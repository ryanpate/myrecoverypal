# Cold-outreach wave 4 — treatment centers — drafted 2026-09-09

The three Illinois treatment centers found to publish real inboxes in the
September sweep (see `prospect-list.md`). No overlap with waves 1–3.

**Different pitch from waves 1–3.** Those sold the Court Compliance tier to
referral sources whose clients are court-mandated. A treatment center's real
pain is what happens *after discharge* — so these lead with the **free facility
aftercare dashboard** (`/accounts/facility/signup/`: consent-gated at-risk alumni
monitoring, weekly Monday risk digest, nothing to pay). The court report is a
secondary mention, and only where the facility actually serves court-ordered
clients.

**Accuracy guardrails used in this copy** — don't loosen these on follow-ups:
- Staff see engagement and risk trends only, **never journal entries or raw
  check-in notes**, and only for alumni who accepted an invite.
- The dashboard is genuinely free — billing is offline/manual, so don't imply a
  price or a trial.
- Signup creates a `pending` facility; a work-email verification link activates it.
