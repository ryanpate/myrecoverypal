## 1. ILAPSC — Illinois Association of Problem-Solving Courts

**To:** ilapsc@ilapsc.org
**Subject:** A way for your member courts to verify meeting-attendance cards

Hi there,

I'm Ryan Pate, founder of MyRecoveryPal, a recovery app built in Rochester, IL.
I'm writing to ILAPSC rather than to individual courts because the problem I
want to describe is one every problem-solving court seems to share.

When a participant turns in a handwritten AA or NA attendance card, there's
usually no fast way to confirm the signature is real.

We built a fix for that. The participant logs each meeting they attend and
generates a PDF attendance report carrying a cryptographic fingerprint. Anyone
holding that printout can paste the fingerprint at a public page on our site and
confirm in about 30 seconds — no account, no cost, no case details exposed — that
the document hasn't been altered since it was generated. Self-reported entries
are labeled as self-reported right on the report, so it never claims more
certainty than it has. It's program-neutral by design (AA, NA, CA, SMART, Refuge,
LifeRing and secular), since courts can't constitutionally require a 12-step
program.

How verification works, on one page:
https://www.myrecoverypal.com/for-probation-officers/

Two questions for you:

1. Would this be worth putting in front of your members — a newsletter mention,
   a conference table, or whatever format you'd consider appropriate?
2. Before that: what would a report like this need before an Illinois court
   would actually accept it? I'd rather build to what your members need than
   guess at it.

Nothing for a court or department to buy or set up — the participant subscribes
on their own ($29.99/mo) and the court just receives cleaner documentation.

Thank you for the work you do,
