# Intergroup feed request — template

Reusable template for asking an AA intergroup / central office for their
Meeting Guide feed. Coverage expansion means sending many of these.

## Why it is written this way

**Tradition 6** is the whole game: an AA group "ought never endorse, finance,
or lend the A.A. name to any related facility or outside enterprise."
MyRecoveryPal is an outside enterprise with paid tiers. If the email does not
address that immediately and honestly, the answer is no — and it should be.

So the template:

1. **Leads with the disclaimer**, not the ask. We are not AA, not affiliated,
   and are not asking for endorsement.
2. **Discloses the Court Compliance tier up front.** It is a paid feature
   directly adjacent to meeting attendance. Anyone can find it in thirty
   seconds; concealing it would be worse than mentioning it.
3. **Names the exact artefact** — the "Public Data Source" link in TSML's Feed
   Management panel. Whoever maintains the site recognises that phrase, which
   makes it a two-minute reply rather than a research project.
4. **Cites their own documentation** — Meeting Guide's "How to provide meeting
   listings to another entity" describes exactly this. We are asking for
   something normal, not inventing a request.
5. **Offers an easy no**, and an easy removal later. Volunteers should not feel
   cornered.
6. **Stays short.** These are unpaid volunteers.

Do not add marketing language, user counts, or anything that reads as a pitch.

## Template

> **Subject:** Meeting list feed — request from a non-AA site
>
> Hello,
>
> I run MyRecoveryPal, a recovery website. To be clear from the start: we're
> not affiliated with AA, we're not asking for any endorsement, and I'm not
> asking anyone to lend the AA name to us. If this request isn't something
> you're comfortable with, a one-line no is a completely fine answer.
>
> We list AA meetings so people can find them, currently for [EXISTING AREAS].
> People search us for [THIS AREA] regularly and we have nothing to show them.
> I'd like to fix that, and I'd rather ask you than scrape your site.
>
> If you're willing: could you send the **Public Data Source** link from the
> Feed Management section of your 12 Step Meeting List plugin? That's the same
> JSON feed described in Meeting Guide's "How to provide meeting listings to
> another entity."
>
> How we'd use it:
>
> - Refreshed weekly, so listings stay current
> - Every meeting page credits [ENTITY] as the source and links back to you
> - Each listing shows a "last verified" date, and tells people to call ahead
> - Meeting listings are free on our site and always will be
> - Remove us any time and we'll drop the feed the same day
>
> One thing I want to state plainly rather than have you find later: we do have
> paid features, including a tool that lets court-ordered people log their own
> meeting attendance and generate a report for a probation officer. That's
> something courts require and AA groups understandably don't want to be in the
> middle of. It records what the person tells us — it never implies AA verified
> anything, and no AA entity is named as attesting to attendance.
>
> Happy to answer anything, or to drop it if you'd prefer we didn't list your
> meetings at all.
>
> Thanks for the work you do,
> Ryan Pate
> ryan@trymyrecoverypal.com
> myrecoverypal.com
