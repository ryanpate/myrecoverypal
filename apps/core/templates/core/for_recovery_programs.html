Meeting attendance for sober living homes and DUI programs

If your residents or clients have to attend AA, NA or other recovery meetings, you probably collect paper sign-in sheets. They get lost, forgotten and occasionally forged. Here’s a way to check attendance that costs your program nothing.

How it works

1
They log each meeting on their phone. Name, date, time and place take about 20 seconds. Logging is free and unlimited.
2
The meeting chair confirms it. The member shows a one-time QR code and the chair or secretary scans it on their own phone. At online meetings, they paste a link in the chat. A member can’t confirm their own attendance.
3
You get a PDF report. It lists every meeting for the period you asked for, marks each one Chair-confirmed or Self-reported, and totals them against the weekly requirement. They can email it to you from the app.
4
You can check it’s genuine. Each report carries a fingerprint code. Enter it on our public verification page and it confirms the report hasn’t been edited. No account needed.

Who uses it

Sober living and recovery homes

House managers checking the weekly meeting requirement without chasing sheets on Sunday night.

DUI and DWI programs

Clients whose program or court order includes self-help meeting attendance alongside classes.

Treatment aftercare and IOP

Counselors who ask clients to attend outside meetings between sessions.

What the report can and can’t tell you

  • Chair-confirmed entries were confirmed by someone at the meeting, on a different device, within a few hours of it. The report shows their name, role and the time.
  • Self-reported entries are the member’s own word and are labeled that way. Editing a confirmed entry removes its confirmation.
  • The chair sees only the member’s first name and last initial, which respects meeting anonymity.
  • It covers AA, NA, CA, SMART Recovery, LifeRing, Refuge Recovery and other programs equally.

Getting started

There’s no signup for your program. Give residents or clients the handout below, or send them the link.

For residents and clients: log your meetings on your phone

  1. Go to myrecoverypal.com/court-ordered-meeting-tracker and create a free account.
  2. After each meeting, tap Log Meeting.
  3. Show the QR code to the chair or secretary so they can confirm it on their phone.
  4. When asked for proof, create a report and email it from the app.
