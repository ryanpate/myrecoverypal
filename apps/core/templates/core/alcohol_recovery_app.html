More Than a Simple Sobriety Counter

While counting sober days is important, research shows that social connection is one of the strongest predictors of long-term sobriety. MyRecoveryPal combines day tracking with a full social network designed specifically for the recovery community.

How Your Brain Heals in Alcohol Recovery

One of the most powerful things you can understand about sobriety is what's happening to your brain's reward system. Alcohol floods the brain's dopamine pathways for years—and recovery is the slow, steady process of those pathways returning to normal. This is why cravings fade over time and why early recovery feels hard even when nothing externally is wrong. Our guide on dopamine detox and addiction recovery explains the brain science in plain language, so you know what to expect and why staying sober gets easier.
