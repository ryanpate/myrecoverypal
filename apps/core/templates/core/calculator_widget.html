What your visitors get

  • Their exact days sober or clean, counted in their own time zone.
  • The same count in years, months and days.
  • The date of their next milestone.

What it does not do

  • No tracking. The widget sets no cookies and loads no analytics, ads or fonts.
  • Nothing is stored. The date a visitor types never leaves their browser.
  • No signup for you or your visitors, and no cost.
