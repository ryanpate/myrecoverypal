How to make it to January 31

  1. Decide your why. Better sleep, saving money, or seeing how you feel. Write it down; you’ll see it on your pledge each day.
  2. Clear the house before January 1, and stock something you’ll actually enjoy drinking instead.
  3. Plan for the hard moments. Fridays, work events and bad days are when most people slip. Decide now what you’ll do and say.
  4. Tell someone. One person who knows makes it far easier to keep going.
  5. If you slip, keep going. One drink doesn’t cancel the days you’ve done. Pledge again tomorrow.

What people notice after a month without alcohol

Everyone is different, but many people who take a month off report sleeping better, having more energy, feeling clearer in the morning and spending noticeably less money. Some find it easy; some find it much harder than they expected. Both are worth knowing. If you want to see your own numbers, the sobriety calculator shows days, money saved and more.

If you drink heavily every day, talk to a doctor before you stop. Stopping suddenly can cause alcohol withdrawal, which can be dangerous. Signs include shaking, sweating, anxiety or feeling sick when you go without a drink. In the US, the SAMHSA helpline is free and open 24/7 at 1-800-662-4357. In a crisis, call or text 988.

Found January harder than you expected?

That’s useful to know, and you’re not alone. Many people who start with Dry January keep going. You can keep pledging after January 31, try an online AA meeting today, or find a meeting near you.

Frequently asked questions

What is Dry January?

Dry January is a challenge to drink no alcohol from January 1 to January 31. People do it to reset after the holidays, to see how they feel without alcohol, or to take a first step toward drinking less for good.

Is it safe to stop drinking suddenly?

For most people, yes. But if you drink heavily every day, stopping suddenly can cause alcohol withdrawal, which can be dangerous. If you get shaky, sweaty, anxious or sick when you go without a drink, talk to a doctor before you stop. In the US you can also call the SAMHSA helpline at 1-800-662-4357, free and confidential, 24/7.

What should I do if I drink during Dry January?

Don’t quit the month. One drink doesn’t cancel the days you’ve already done. Notice what led to it, plan for that moment next time, and take tomorrow’s pledge.

Can I start Dry January late?

Yes. Join any day in January and pledge from that day on. You can also sign up now and the tracker will start on January 1.
