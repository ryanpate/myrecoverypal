What the year holds

Recovery isn't one big day. It's a lot of ordinary ones, and a few very hard ones. This is for both.

  • The first nights
    Something to press play on

    Sleep wind-downs, morning intentions and a body scan, on top of the craving and grounding audio that is always free.

  • 2am, any night
    Someone to talk it through with

    Anchor, an AI recovery coach, answers at any hour, up to 20 messages a day, and remembers earlier conversations. It isn't therapy. It's company in the gap before morning.

  • Every morning
    Five minutes with a plan

    Every guided program in full: First 30 Days, plus two-week tracks for alcohol, opioids, stimulants, cannabis and gambling. One short lesson and one small action a day.

  • A missed day
    A streak that forgives

    One missed day a week won't reset their pledge or check-in streak, and 90 days of mood and craving trends show them how far they've come.

  • Day 30, 90, 365
    A medallion on the day it counts

    Their milestone medallion arrives by email at 7, 30, 90 and 180 days and on every anniversary, in HD and as a short video.

  • And for you
    A seat beside them, if they want you there

    Premium includes one Supporter seat. If they invite you, you can follow their progress, send encouragement, and take the 14-day Family & Friends course, at no extra cost. It is always their choice.

Before you give it

It stays between you and them

We never contact the person you're buying for. You hand over the card or send the link yourself, in your own words, when the moment is right.

It never turns into a bill

No card is attached to their account and nothing renews. A week before the year ends we let them know, and everything in the free app stays theirs.

They already use MyRecoveryPal?

The gift adds a year of Premium to the account they have. If they're new, they create a free account in under a minute and the year starts then.

Not ready yet?

The link doesn't expire. The year starts on the day they redeem it, not the day you bought it.
