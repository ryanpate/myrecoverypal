Delay & Wait

10 minutes

Tell yourself you'll wait 10 minutes before acting on the craving. Often, the urge will pass. Repeat as needed.

🫁

Deep Breathing

5 minutes

Practice 4-7-8 breathing: inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7, exhale for 8. This calms your nervous system.

✓
🎯

Distract & Engage

15+ minutes

Shift focus with an enjoyable activity: read, listen to music, watch a video, or start a creative project.

✓
🌊

Urge Surfing

5-10 minutes

Notice the craving as a wave—observe it rising and falling without judgment. You don't have to act on it.

✓
💧

Hydrate & Nourish

2 minutes

Drink water slowly and mindfully. Proper hydration can help reduce physical tension and cravings.

✓
🏃

Move Your Body

10-20 minutes

Go for a walk, stretch, or do jumping jacks. Physical movement shifts your energy and focus.

✓
🧘

Practice Mindfulness

5 minutes

Ground yourself: name 5 things you see, 4 you hear, 3 you touch, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.

✓
📞

Reach Out

Varies

Call or text a friend, sponsor, or support group member. Connection is powerful in recovery.

✓
💭

Positive Self-Talk

2-5 minutes

Use affirmations: "I am strong," "This feeling will pass," "I choose my recovery."

✓
📝

Journal It Out

10 minutes

Write about the trigger, your feelings, and which coping strategy you used. Track your progress.
